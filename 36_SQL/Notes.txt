================WHERE Clause=============
1. Arithmetic operators -> + , - , * , / , %
2. Comparison operators -> = , != , > , < , <= ,>=
3. Logical operators -> AND , OR , NOT , IN , BETWEEN , ALL , LIKE , ANY
4. Bitwise operators -> & , |


select *
from user
where age>25;

from user
where age>25
limit 1;
